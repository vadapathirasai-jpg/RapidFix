DYNAMODB TABLES (7 Total):

1. Users
   ├── PK: userId
   ├── GSI: email (unique query)
   ├── GSI: role (list by role)
   └── Fields: credentials, profile, timestamps

2. Technicians
   ├── PK: technicianId
   ├── GSI: userId
   ├── GSI: specialization (filter)
   ├── GSI: verificationStatus (admin view)
   └── Fields: profile, skills, rates, availability

3. Bookings
   ├── PK: bookingId
   ├── GSI: customerId (user's bookings)
   ├── GSI: technicianId (tech's jobs)
   ├── GSI: status (filter by state)
   ├── GSI: createdAt (time-based queries)
   └── Fields: service, location, price, timeline

4. Reviews
   ├── PK: reviewId
   ├── GSI: technicianId (tech's ratings)
   ├── GSI: customerId (customer's reviews)
   ├── GSI: bookingId (booking feedback)
   └── Fields: rating (1-5), comment, timestamps

5. Services
   ├── PK: serviceId
   ├── GSI: category (browse by type)
   └── Fields: name, price, description, icon

6. Verifications
   ├── PK: verificationId
   ├── GSI: technicianId
   ├── GSI: status (pending/approved/rejected)
   └── Fields: documents, approval info

7. Notifications
   ├── PK: notificationId
   ├── GSI: userId (user's notifications)
   └── Fields: type, message, read status
